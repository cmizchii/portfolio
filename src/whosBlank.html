01 The problem

The game night that didn't happen.

Four of us wanted to play the TikTok word-imposter game. But to set it up, one person has to pick the secret word, give everyone theirs, and know who the imposter is, which means that person can't really play. We couldn't find an app that just did it for us, so that night we didn't play at all.

✍️
Someone keeps the word

One player writes the words down, and instantly knows everyone's secret.

🪑
That person sits out

The host can't be the imposter or a guesser, so a 4-player group becomes 3.

🎲
No way to randomize

Picking who's the imposter fairly, by hand, with nobody knowing, is basically impossible.

😴
The vibe dies

By the time setup is sorted, the momentum, and the night, is gone.

02 The idea

Let the phone keep the secret, so everyone can play.

One phone becomes the neutral host. It picks the word, hides one player as the Blank, and gets passed around the circle. Each person peeks at their own screen and hands it on. No host, no paper, no one left out.

03 How the game works

Describe your word. Sniff out the Blank.

An in-app guide teaches the rules in a few taps, so a first-timer at the table gets it instantly.

1
Everyone secretly gets the same word, except the Blank, who only sees “Blank.”
2
Going round the circle, each player describes the word vaguely enough not to give it away.
3
The Blank bluffs to blend in. Then everyone votes on who they think it is.
4
Still unsure? Call the Hot Seat and grill one suspect for 30s without revealing the word.
04 Setting up a round

Two screens and you're in.

Add whoever's playing, flip on the modes you want for this round, and hit start. Inputs are big, tappable pills made to be passed around a loud room.

1Add the players

Type a name, tap add, repeat. A delete button on each pill keeps editing quick.

2Pick your modes

Yellow toggles for Hot Seat, Joker and No Imposter, mixed for a fresh round every time.

05 The reveal moment

“Are you Sarah?” Pass, peek, pass on.

A cover screen names the next player before showing anything, so the phone can travel safely. Tap, see your word, hand it on. The Blank gets the same flow, but their card simply reads “Blank,” in red, so they know they're it.

06 Modes that change the game

Three twists, endless rounds.

Each mode rewires who's lying to whom. Stack them for chaos, or play it straight. The table decides before every round.

🃏
Joker

A player who knows the word is secretly named the Joker. Their goal: act suspicious and get voted as the imposter on purpose. If the group falls for it, the Joker wins.

Sabotage from the inside
🔥
Hot Seat

When the group can't decide, a majority vote puts one suspect in the Hot Seat for 30 seconds of rapid, vague questioning, without ever revealing the word, to test if they really know it.

Pressure-test a suspect
🫥
No Imposter

The cruel one: everyone gets the word and nobody is the Blank, but they don't know that. The table has to find the confidence to vote “there's no imposter.”

Paranoia, fully weaponized
07 Dialling it in

Defaults you set once, tweaks per round.

Settings remember your group's favourite setup, while small dropdowns and sliders fine-tune how each mode behaves, like the Joker's style or how long the round timer runs.

Reflection & next steps

What I'd carry forward.

Designing for a phone that gets passed around taught me to treat the cover screen as a real safety feature, not a transition. One careless tap can spoil a whole round.

Starting from a frustration my friends and I actually had kept every decision honest: if a screen didn't help us get to playing faster, it didn't make the cut.

Next: playtest the modes
Get groups playing Joker and No Imposter to balance how often each side wins.
Next: scoring & rounds
Add a running scoreboard so a session becomes a best-of-five, not one-and-done.
Next: word packs
Themed decks for food, movies and spicy nights, plus a way for groups to add their own.
